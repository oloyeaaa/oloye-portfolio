# Google pages roadmap (set 3 Nov 2026)

*Oloye, 3 Nov: a few new pages every day, maybe five, not all at once. Each one helps oloye.co.uk show up on Google.*

**The message on every page:** automation and systems for small businesses, saving time, saving money and making money. Use "task" or "workflow", never "job". The socials line ("stop losing the people who find you") never goes on these pages.

## The pace
- **Week 1:** 2 to 3 pages a day, while the first pages set the template and the checks.
- **After that:** up to 5 a day, as long as every page passes the check.
- **Each day's flow:** the Research Analyst picks the keywords, the Copywriter writes the pages, the Web Developer builds them on a preview, the Chief of Staff checks them, and Oloye says yes. Then they go live, and the sitemap and internal links get updated.
- **The quality bar:** every page has its own real tasks, examples and FAQ. No near-copy pages with only the trade name swapped, because Google marks those down. If there's not enough real material, the page waits.

## The page types (in order)
1. **Who-it's-for pages.** Coaches, content creators, small business, then the trades: barbers (VOB Cuts is the real example), plumbers, cleaners, personal trainers, salons, electricians, accountants and more.
2. **Task pages.** One for each of the "10 tasks to automate first", e.g. invoice reminders, or an auto reply to website enquiries.
3. **Free tools.** The "How much is admin costing you?" calculator (built from the home page slider). The lead leak check is already live.
4. **Free templates.** An Airtable lead tracker, a follow-up email set, Claude prompts for small business. Each one asks for an email.
5. **Tool pages.** Google Forms to Airtable, Make vs Zapier for small business, Claude for small business.
6. **Local pages.** Business automation in Kent, Medway and Chatham, plus a free Google Business Profile.
7. **Join them up.** Each Wednesday blog post links to its matching hub page, and the hub page links back.
8. **The basics.** Google Search Console set up and checked, so we grow from real search numbers.

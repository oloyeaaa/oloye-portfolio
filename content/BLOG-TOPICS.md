# Blog post topics (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)

Three posts a week since 5 Nov 2026 (Oloye: "Yes, three a week"). The blog writer skill takes the `next` row, or if there's none, the `planned` row dated for the post it's writing. Oloye can add, reorder or strike anything. Each topic says what's real (so nothing gets invented) and a keyword to test first.

**Status:** `next` (the next one to draft), `planned` (a set day), `drafted` (draft branch made, waiting for the check and his yes), `idea`, `done` (with date and link), `held` (needs Oloye).

**Built?:** Yes = we can say "we built". Partly = write it as a how-to, with no "we built", and say only the part that's true. Rows that aren't built at all come off the list until they are (see "Saved for when it's built").

## The rule: the blog follows the campaign (Oloye, 1 Nov 2026)

*"Whatever system we're talking about within that campaign period, we'll talk about the methods, system inside our system... pick one and write about it."*

- While a campaign runs, **posts are parts of that campaign's system**, keyword-researched and SEO-optimised like any other post. When the next campaign starts, the list is rewritten the same way.
- **Now (6 Nov 2026 to 27 Jan 2027): the email system.** It has 7 steps (`content/story-email-system/system-doc/sop/`): free thing, sign-up doors, the pipes, one list, welcome email, weekly story email, morning follow-up. Plus the parts inside it: the story bank, comment keyword to DM, checking an email before it goes, and tagging by interest.
- **Three a week needs more than the system's parts (5 Nov; he's happy to use any of the systems):** the rows with no system step are other real builds, across the 5 categories, with Leads as the bulk. Every Admin post ties back to getting time back. No new builds: posts use what already exists (`BUILT-SO-FAR.md`). No content-making tips (hooks, slides, camera, editing) while the campaign runs. The one exception is the AI team series below, approved by Oloye on 6 Nov.
- **Written for coaches and small business owners**, in plain words, with the blog links rule (3+ internal, 2+ external).
- **Every post has one free offer, set by its category, and ends on it** (Oloye, 6 Nov 2026; `src/lib/postOffers.ts`): Leads gets the Lead leak check, Tools and AI gets TOOLS, Content gets LOOP, Admin and invoices and Clients get the 10 tasks checklist.
- **Every post gets one of the 5 categories** (Content added 6 Nov 2026): **Leads** (catching and answering enquiries), **Clients** (onboarding, folders, reviews), **Admin and invoices** (task emails, chasing payment, repeat work), **Tools and AI** (how-tos with Make, Airtable and Claude) or **Content** (turning posts into people on your own list). The Category column below says which.
- **Only what's true and built.** Check the row on the day. Never mention his job.
- **Titles come from search research first** (Oloye, 6 Nov 2026): Google Trends through Firecrawl, or if Firecrawl is out, Google suggestions, vidIQ or a web search. UK Trends still gets checked when the post is drafted.
- **The AI team series (2 to 21 Dec, Content):** a post per agent, then the full system. Each post shows the real agent working and its real results only. Research: `content/blog-ai-team-series-keywords.md`.

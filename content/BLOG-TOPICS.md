# Blog post topics (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)

Three posts a week since 5 Nov 2026 (Oloye: "Yes, three a week"). The blog writer skill takes the `next` row, or if there's none, the `planned` row dated for the post it's writing. Oloye can add, reorder or strike anything. Each topic says what's real (so nothing gets invented) and a keyword to test first.

**Status:** `next` (the next one to draft), `planned` (a set day), `drafted` (draft branch made, waiting for the check and his yes), `idea`, `done` (with date and link), `held` (needs Oloye).

**Built?:** Yes = we can say "we built". Partly = say only the part that's true. **NO** = not built, so write it as a general how-to with no "we built", or swap it with a later row.

## The rule: the blog follows the campaign (Oloye, 1 Nov 2026)

*"Whatever system we're talking about within that campaign period, we'll talk about the methods, system inside our system... pick one and write about it."*

- While a campaign runs, **posts are parts of that campaign's system**, keyword-researched and SEO-optimised like any other post. When the next campaign starts, the list is rewritten the same way.
- **Now (6 Nov 2026 to 27 Jan 2027): the email system.** It has 7 steps (`content/story-email-system/system-doc/sop/`): free thing, sign-up doors, the pipes, one list, welcome email, weekly story email, morning follow-up. Plus the parts inside it: the story bank, comment keyword to DM, checking an email before it goes, and tagging by interest.
- **Three a week needs more than the system's 12 parts (5 Nov):** the rows with no system step are other real builds, across the 4 categories. No new builds: posts use what already exists (`BUILT-SO-FAR.md`). Each one still comes back to not losing people or getting time back. No content-making tips (hooks, slides, camera, editing) while the campaign runs.
- **Written for coaches and small business owners**, in plain words, with the blog links rule (3+ internal, 2+ external).
- **Every post ends on the free thing.** From 27 Nov, if oloye.co.uk/100 is live, it ends on /100 and the 0 to 100 Kit instead.
- **Every post gets one of the 4 categories** (set 3 Nov 2026): **Leads** (catching and answering enquiries), **Clients** (onboarding, folders, reviews), **Admin and invoices** (task emails, chasing payment, repeat work) or **Tools and AI** (how-tos with Make, Airtable and Claude). The Category column below says which.
- **Only what's true and built.** Check the row on the day. Never mention his job.

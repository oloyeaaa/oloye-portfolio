# Wednesday post topics

The blog writer skill takes the first `next`, then the top `idea`. Oloye can add, reorder or strike anything. Each topic says what's real (so nothing gets invented) and a keyword to test first.

**Status:** `next` (the coming Wednesday), `planned` (a set Wednesday), `idea`, `done` (with date and link), `held` (needs Oloye).

## The rule: the blog follows the campaign (Oloye, 1 Nov 2026)

*"Whatever system we're talking about within that campaign period, we'll talk about the methods, system inside our system... pick one and write about it."*

- While a campaign runs, **every Wednesday post is one part of that campaign's system**, keyword-researched and SEO-optimised like any other post. When the next campaign starts, the list is rewritten the same way.
- **Now (4 Nov 2026 to 27 Jan 2027): the email system.** It has 7 steps (`content/story-email-system/system-doc/sop/`): free thing, sign-up doors, the pipes, one list, welcome email, weekly story email, morning follow-up. Plus the parts inside it: the story bank, comment keyword to DM, checking an email before it goes, and tagging by interest.
- **Match the Wednesday keyword Reel** where there is one, so the post and the blog back each other up.
- **Written for coaches and small business owners**, in plain words, with the blog links rule (3+ internal, 2+ external).
- **Every post ends on the free thing.** From 27 Nov, if oloye.co.uk/100 is live, it ends on /100 and the 0 to 100 Kit instead.
- **Only what's true and built.** If a part isn't built by its Wednesday, write it as a general how-to (no "we built") or swap it with a later row. Never mention his job.

## Why onboarding slips

The first week with a new client sets the tone. But onboarding happens when you're busiest, because you've just won the work. So the welcome email goes out a day late. The form gets forgotten. You chase for the same details twice.

Accountants know this well. Since Making Tax Digital, **60%** of UK practices say they spend more time per client, and **55%** type up paper records for clients. *([Bright Software Group](https://www.internationalaccountingbulletin.com/news/mtd-increases-workload-for-60-of-accountancy-practices-bright-finds/), a survey of hundreds of UK practices, Aug to Sep 2026.)*

## An automated client onboarding process

1. **They say yes.** You tick "Won" next to their name in your client list in [Airtable](https://www.airtable.com/). Or a payment or signed contract does it for you.
2. **A welcome email goes out from your Gmail** with what happens next and one link: the onboarding form.
3. **They fill in the client onboarding form.** Their answers land on their row in the list, so you never type them in.
4. **A folder is made for them in Google Drive**, with your standard documents copied in. ([How the client folders work](/automate/client-folders).)
5. **A link to book the first call goes out**, and a reminder the day before.
6. **Haven't filled in the form after a few days?** A gentle nudge goes out. Then it stops.

[Make](https://www.make.com/) joins the steps together. It's the tool that passes information from one app to the next.

## Client onboarding checklist

What every new client should get, in order:

- A welcome email within the hour
- One form with everything you need (no back-and-forth)
- Their own folder, with the documents they'll need
- A link to book the first call, and a reminder
- A short "what happens next" so they know the plan
- A nudge if something's missing

If any of these depend on you remembering, that's the step to automate first.

## What a slow start costs

A new client who waits two days for a welcome email starts to wonder. A client you chase three times for the same details starts the work annoyed. And every hour you spend copying details manually is an hour you're not paid for.

## The tasks nobody sees

Saving each new enquiry. Sending the same welcome email again. Chasing the invoice. Remembering to ask for a review. Each task only takes a few minutes. Together they take your week.

In one UK survey, small business owners said they lose about 8 hours a week to repetitive admin. When asked which task they'd most like to automate, invoicing and chasing payment came first by a wide margin. *([HeyBRB](https://www.electricaltimes.co.uk/uk-tradespeople-losing-ten-working-weeks-a-year-to-admin-they-could-automate-survey-finds/), an AI automation firm, 167 UK owner-operators, March 2026.)*

## What small business automation looks like

Here's a workflow we built for our own business first. A new enquiry comes in from the website.

1. **Someone fills in the form on your website.**
2. **Make picks it up straight away.** Make is the tool that passes information from one app to the next.
3. **Their details land in one list in Airtable**, with where they came from and the date.
4. **A reply goes out from your Gmail**, so they hear back while they still care.
5. **Each morning, ask for a short brief.** It lists who's still waiting for a proper reply, with a draft written by Claude for you to check.

No copying names into a spreadsheet. No "I'll reply tonight". ## AI automation for small business, without the hype

AI is a tool inside the workflow, not the point of it. Claude writes first drafts of replies and sorts messages. The rest is simple, dependable tools joined together: a form, a list, an email. You stay in charge of what goes out.

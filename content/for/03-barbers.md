## Why barber no-shows hurt more

You keep the slot free. You turn a walk-in away. They don't show.

Barbers get hit harder than most. On one big booking platform, barbershops had an average no-show rate of **21.16%**, about 1 in 5 bookings. Hair salons were at **3.16%**. *([Fresha](https://professionalbeauty.co.uk/beauty-pros-lost-26m-to-no-shows-this-summer), over 3 million appointments, May to August 2024.)*

## How to reduce no-shows, on their own

1. **They book** in your booking app, or a slot goes into Google Calendar.
2. **Straight away, a confirmation goes out** with the time, the address, and your no-show rule in one plain line.
3. **The day before, a reminder goes out**, with an easy way to cancel or move it.
4. **They cancel?** The slot goes to the first person on your waiting list, so the gap gets filled, not lost.
5. **They don't show?** It's noted against their name. A second no-show, and they get a polite message that next time needs a deposit.

The tools: your booking app or [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/), [Make](https://www.make.com/) to join them up, and one list of clients in [Airtable](https://www.airtable.com/).

## Appointment reminder text and no-show fee message

**The reminder, the day before:**

> Hi [name], see you tomorrow at [time] at [shop]. Can't make it? Reply or tap here to move it: [link]. Thanks, [shop]

**The no-show rule, when they book:**

> Thanks for booking with [shop]. If you can't make it, let us know by [time] the day before, so someone else can have the slot. Missed appointments may be charged [amount].

**After a second no-show:**

> Hi [name], we missed you today. To keep slots fair for everyone, the next booking needs a [amount] deposit. Book here when you're ready: [link]

Short, friendly, and nobody's told off.

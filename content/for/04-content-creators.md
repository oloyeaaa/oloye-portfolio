## Where your week really goes

You have the idea in the shower. Then the work starts. Write the script. Film it again. Cut out the pauses. Make a cover. Write a caption. Do it all again tomorrow.

None of it is hard. It's just a lot of small tasks, done manually, every single time. So the posts slow down, and the ideas sit in your notes app.

## What an AI content creation system looks like

Here's the machine Oloye built for his own posts first. He built it by talking to Claude, with no code.

1. **Every script starts as a rambling voice note.** The machine cuts it down to your own sentences, so it still sounds like you.
2. **The Carousel Studio builds the slides** from one design file, so every post looks like yours.
3. **The Video Studio does the edit.** It trims the pauses, adds big captions and makes the cover.
4. **One checklist runs on every post** before you see it: the hook, the facts, the words.
5. **One table holds every post,** from idea to posted, so nothing gets lost.

You still have the idea, and you still say yes. The machine does the heavy parts in between.

## Batch content creation, without a team

You don't need an editor and a designer to post every day. Oloye films a week of talks on one Saturday morning, from a dated plan, and the machine turns each one into a finished post. Every clip goes into one Drive folder and gets used, so nothing filmed is wasted.

## AI is the tool, not the point

Claude does the drafting and the editing. You stay in charge of what goes out. And the posts are only half of it: the people who find you need somewhere to go, so you can reach them again without hoping the app shows them your next video.

## 1. Engine 01: The Customer Language Mining Workflow

Never brainstorm customer pain from scratch. Instead, let AI do the heavy lifting of reading thousands of customer reviews:

### The Claude / Gemini Prompt Architecture:
```text
You are an expert qualitative researcher specializing in Eugene Schwartz buyer psychology.
Below are 40 1-star and 2-star reviews from competitors in our industry.

Analyze these reviews and extract:
1. The 5 most repeated emotional frustrations (in the customer's exact raw words).
2. The specific "2:00 AM bleeding-neck headache" they are trying to eliminate.
3. The exact slang and terminology they use to describe their daily workflow.

Do not use corporate jargon. Output pure customer language.
```

In 30 seconds, you have a goldmine of validated customer phrases to build your next video hook or landing page headline around.

---

## 2. Engine 02: The 10th-Grade Script Architect

To avoid the robotic "AI voice", train your AI system on strict conversational constraints:

* **Rule 1:** Speak like an experienced practitioner talking at a whiteboard with a friend.
* **Rule 2:** Eliminate all corporate buzzwords (*"synergy", "paradigm shift", "empower"*).
* **Rule 3:** Use short, rhythmic sentences with real-world analogies.

---

## 3. Engine 03: The One-to-Many Repurposing Pipeline

Whenever you record a video (or draft a foundational teardown):
1. **Raw Video Take** → Transcribe with Descript or Whisper.
2. **AI Markdown Transformer** → Clean up spoken tangents, format into a high-SEO blog post with headers, tables, and key takeaways.
3. **Shorts & Social Micro-Content** → Extract 3 standalone signal posts for LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok.

The biggest copywriting mistake solopreneurs make is trying to write copy from their own imagination.

They sit in front of a blank screen, trying to think: *"What catchy slogan will make people want to buy my software?"*

Here is the secret of the world's highest-paid copywriters: **Great copy is not written. Great copy is assembled.**

Your future customers have already written your entire landing page, your email subject lines, and your video hooks for you. You just need to know where to find their words.

---

## ⛏️ Where to Mine Pure Customer Language

When customers are happy, they write polite, generic reviews (*"Great tool, easy to use"*). That copy is useless.

When customers are frustrated and angry, their emotional filter drops. They write raw, visceral, 100% authentic descriptions of their daily struggle.

Here are the 3 best goldmines for language mining:

### 1. 1-Star & 2-Star Competitor Reviews on G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot
Look for what the market incumbents fail to do.
* *Customer quote:* *"I spent three days trying to set up their custom integration, and their support took 48 hours to reply with a broken link."*
* **Your Winning Headline:** *"The automated lead engine that takes 5 minutes to set up, not 3 days."*

### 2. Reddit Communities & Niche Forums
Search subreddits like `r/SaaS`, `r/Entrepreneur`, or `r/solopreneur` for terms like:
* *"I hate when..."*
* *"Why is it so hard to..."*
* *"Does anyone have a workaround for..."*

### 3. Recorded Sales & Discovery Calls
Listen for the phrase right after they sigh: *"Honestly, the most annoying thing is..."* Whatever follows that sentence is worth thousands in copy value.

## 🪞 The Mirror Effect: Reading Your Prospect's Mind

When a potential buyer visits your website or watches your video and hears the exact words they said to their co-founder yesterday:

1. They immediately believe you understand their problem better than anyone else.
2. They assume your solution must be the exact fix they need.
3. They don't feel like you are selling to them — **they feel like you are reading their mind.**
